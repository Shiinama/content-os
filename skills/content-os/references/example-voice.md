# Voice Profile

## Writing Samples
- [2026-01-02] "We spent two months polishing onboarding animations. Churn didn't move. Then we deleted one form field and it dropped 9%. I keep relearning the same lesson: users don't want a nicer path, they want a shorter one."
- [2026-01-20] "Everyone asks which model to use. Almost nobody asks which 20 lines of context to give it. The second question is worth 10x the first and costs nothing."

## Tone Preferences
- Direct claims first, hedging only where genuinely uncertain
- Dry humor allowed, exclamation marks rare
- Prefers concrete numbers and named examples over adjectives
- Short sentences; reads aloud well

## Vocabulary
- Uses: ship, lever, default, moat, friction, judgment call
- Avoids: leverage (as a verb), synergy, delve, journey (for product flows), empower

## AI-tell Blacklist
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "Let's dive in" / "Buckle up"
- "It's worth noting that" / "值得注意的是"
- "In conclusion" / "总的来说"
- "game-changer", "unlock the power of"
- Rule-of-three adjective stacks ("faster, smarter, better")
