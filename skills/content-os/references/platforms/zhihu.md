# Zhihu (知乎)

Default output language: Chinese.

## Audience & mindset
Readers who came to understand something properly. They tolerate length if the reasoning is rigorous, and they punish hand-waving. Credibility is earned inside the text: evidence, mechanism, first-hand experience.

## Format & length
Answer (回答) or article (文章). 800 to 3000 characters typical. Structured paragraphs with clear logical progression; subheadings and bold for the skeleton in longer pieces. Links and citations are allowed and add credibility.

## Visuals
- Articles: one 16:9 title image optional. Mechanism diagrams (flows, structures, comparisons) fit Zhihu's show-your-work culture better than mood covers; answers take no images by default.
- Inline diagrams at 16:9 or 4:3 only where a mechanism is genuinely clearer drawn than written.

## Structure norms
- Open with the conclusion or the counterintuitive reframe in the first 2 sentences; Zhihu readers decide from the folded preview.
- Then the mechanism: why this is true, what everyone gets wrong, where the boundary conditions are.
- Evidence layer: data, cases, personal practice with specifics.
- Close by scoping the claim honestly: when it holds, when it does not. Scoped claims outperform grand ones here.

## Tone & information density
Rational, first-person expert but not lecturing: 专业而不装. High density; cut anecdotes that do not carry logical weight. Show your work: numbers, sources, trade-offs. Admitting uncertainty at the right spots increases trust.

## Hooks & titles
For articles: titles that state the reframe ("churn survey 测到的不是流失原因，是事后合理化"). For answers: the question is the title; your first two sentences are the hook. Counterintuitive plus rigorous is the winning combination.

## CTA norms
Weak CTA culture; 赞同 flows from quality. At most a closing line inviting discussion on a specific disagreement. Self-promotion only in a restrained footer if at all.

## Taboos & failure modes
- Marketing tone or thin listicles get downvoted and buried.
- Claims without mechanism or evidence attract hostile top comments.
- Emotional bait that works on other platforms reads as low-quality here.
- Walls of text without logical signposting lose readers regardless of quality.

## Pre-publish checklist
- Conclusion visible within the first 2 sentences
- Mechanism explained, not just asserted
- At least one concrete piece of evidence with specifics
- Claim explicitly scoped (when it holds, when it does not)
- Tone check: rigorous, no hype words
