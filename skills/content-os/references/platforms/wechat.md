# WeChat Official Account (公众号)

Default output language: Chinese.

## Audience & mindset
Subscribers who chose to follow; they open articles from a feed of titles or from shares in group chats. Reading happens on a phone, often in fragments. They expect depth from an account they follow, but the title and the first screen decide whether they read at all.

## Format & length
Long-form article. Sweet spot 1500 to 4000 characters; shorter reads thin for the medium, much longer needs exceptional structure. Short paragraphs (2 to 4 lines on mobile), subheadings every 300 to 500 characters, generous whitespace. Images or dividers to break long stretches.

## Visuals
- Cover image is mandatory before publish: it is the feed thumbnail and the share card. Generate at 16:9 with the subject and any on-image text held in the vertical middle band; the feed crops toward 2.35:1, so state the crop guidance when delivering.
- Inline images optional, at most one per major section — the "images or dividers to break long stretches" line above is their job description. Generate at 16:9 or 4:3 only where a section genuinely needs one.

## Structure norms
- First screen (before any scrolling) must establish the tension: what question this article answers and why the reader should care. No slow warm-ups.
- One core judgment per article, developed in 3 to 5 movements; each subheading advances the argument rather than labeling a topic.
- End with a conclusion that compresses the argument into one repeatable sentence; readers share sentences, not paragraphs.

## Tone & information density
First person, opinionated, with the reasoning shown. WeChat rewards essays that feel like a sharp friend thinking out loud, not encyclopedic coverage. Medium density: every section earns its length, but the reader is given room to breathe with examples and restatements.

## Hooks & titles
Title is the single highest-leverage element: 15 to 30 characters, concrete tension or counterintuitive claim beats cleverness. Patterns that work: a strong judgment stated plainly ("退订按钮越难找，你的品牌死得越快"), a specific mistake plus stakes, a question the target reader is already asking. Avoid vague abstractions.

## CTA norms
Native actions: 在看, 点赞, 转发, 关注. One CTA, placed at the end, tied to the content ("如果你也在做订阅产品，转给你的增长同事"). External links do not render as links; route them through 阅读原文 or omit.

## Taboos & failure modes
- Induced sharing or follow-gating is penalized by the platform.
- Clickbait titles that the body cannot cash destroy the account's open rate over time.
- Listicles without a spine read as compilation, not judgment; the account's voice is the moat.
- Do not paste bare URLs in the body.

## Pre-publish checklist
- Title under 30 characters and states a tension or claim
- First screen hooks without scrolling
- Subheadings advance one argument
- One closing sentence worth quoting
- No bare external links; CTA is native and single
