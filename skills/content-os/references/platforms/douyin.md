# Douyin image carousel (抖音图文)

Default output language: Chinese.

## Audience & mindset
Feed swipers who give the cover a fraction of a second. Image carousels (图文) are consumed as swipe-through slides with music; each slide gets 1 to 3 seconds. The cover slide is effectively the title, thumbnail, and hook in one.

## Format & length
3 to 10 generated vertical slides (3:4). Each slide is large text on a clean background or over an image: one point per slide, ideally under 40 characters, big readable font. Plus a caption (under 100 characters) and 3 to 5 topic tags. This profile produces the per-slide on-image text, a filled image prompt per slide, the generated slides themselves, and the caption with tags.

## Visuals
- 3 to 10 slides at 3:4, all generated from one visual template; the fixed 版式 is what makes the swipe rhythm readable.
- Cover slide is mandatory and must stand alone as a muted feed thumbnail.
- One point per slide in big type. "Under 40 characters" is a generation constraint, not a style preference: if a slide's text will not fit in large font, it is two slides.
- Final slide carries the takeaway plus the soft CTA.

## Structure norms
- Slide 1 (cover): the hook as a bold claim, pain, or promise with a number. Must be legible as a feed thumbnail.
- Slides 2 to N-1: one point each, in a fixed rhythm (e.g. 坑1/坑2/坑3, or step by step). Consistent visual pattern across slides.
- Final slide: the takeaway in one line plus a soft follow or save prompt.
- Caption: restate the promise with searchable keywords and tags; do not repeat slide text verbatim.

## Tone & information density
Punchy, spoken-language phrasing: write how people talk, not how they write. Very high compression: every slide is a headline, details live in the caption or comments. Numbers and concrete nouns over abstractions.

## Hooks & titles
Cover patterns that work: 人群+痛点+数字 ("新手做AI产品必踩的3个坑"), before/after, 反常识断言. The cover text must be understandable with zero context while muted.

## CTA norms
Save (收藏) and follow prompts on the final slide only, phrased as value ("怕忘就收藏"). Comment seeding via a question in the caption works better than on slides.

## Taboos & failure modes
- Text-dense slides are skipped; if a slide needs small font, it is two slides.
- Covers that need the caption to make sense never get opened.
- Absolute claims and 违禁词 (最, 第一, 治愈, income promises) risk review; keep claims concrete and modest.
- No external links; do not include URLs anywhere.

## Pre-publish checklist
- Cover text under 20 characters, legible as a thumbnail, hook stands alone
- One point per slide, under 40 characters each, consistent rhythm
- All slides generated at 3:4 from one visual template, cover stands alone
- Final slide carries the takeaway plus one soft CTA
- Caption adds keywords and a comment hook, 3 to 5 tags
