# Xiaohongshu (小红书)

Default output language: Chinese.

## Audience & mindset
Users searching and browsing for useful, relatable experience: how-tos, pitfalls, real results. They save (收藏) what they might need later and trust 真人感 (a real person sharing) over authority. A note is consumed as a swipe-through of slides first; the caption is read second, if at all. The cover slide and title decide the click; the slides decide the save.

## Format & length
Image-carousel note (图文笔记): 3 to 9 generated 3:4 slides carry the content, plus a title (up to 20 characters) and a caption (正文, under 1000 characters, 600 to 800 reads best). The slides are the body: one beat per slide, on-image text under 60 characters. The caption restates the promise, carries the specifics that did not fit on slides and the searchable keywords, and ends with the interaction hook. Short lines, blank lines between beats, emojis as visual rhythm (2 to 6 total, not every line).

## Visuals
- 3 to 9 slides at 3:4, all generated from one visual template so the carousel shares a single 版式; mixing styles mid-carousel reads as spam.
- Cover slide is mandatory and carries the promise: title-grade text, legible as a feed thumbnail.
- Content slides: one point each, on-image text under 60 characters; anything longer belongs in the caption.
- Final slide: the takeaway in one line plus a soft save prompt.

## Structure norms
- Title: outcome or pain + audience + a number where honest ("新手做AI产品的3个坑，我全踩过了"); it doubles as the cover slide's headline.
- Cover slide: restate the promise in one breath, personal and direct.
- Slides 2 to N-1: numbered points or clear beats, one per slide, each with a concrete detail that proves you lived it.
- Final slide: one takeaway line plus an interaction hook.
- Caption: the promise in one opening line, then the supporting specifics (prices, timelines, numbers) with topic keywords worked in naturally, ending with one small question to seed comments.

## Tone & information density
First-person lived experience: 我踩过 / 我试了 / 亲测. Warm, direct, zero lecture tone. High usefulness density but low formality; specifics (prices, timelines, numbers) are what make notes save-worthy — headline versions on the slides, full versions in the caption.

## Hooks & titles
Self-attesting confession, checklists, before/after, 避坑 and 教程 angles all work. The title must match what the slides deliver; the search traffic depends on honest keywords (put the topic words in title and caption naturally).

## CTA norms
收藏 and comments are the currency. Invite saves implicitly by being reference-worthy; ask one small question to seed comments. Follow requests only after value is delivered.

## Taboos & failure modes
- External links are not allowed; do not include URLs on slides or in the caption.
- Absolute claims trip 违禁词 review: avoid 最, 第一, 绝对, 秒杀, 治愈 and medical or income guarantees — on slides too, where review OCR reads them.
- Marketing tone ("宝子们冲鸭" without substance, or obvious ad copy) kills reach and trust.
- Fabricated experience gets dismantled in comments; details you cannot back up will be asked about.
- Text-dense slides do not get swiped through; if a slide needs small font, it is two slides.

## Pre-publish checklist
- Title under 20 characters, keyword-honest, promise matches the slides
- All slides generated at 3:4 from one visual template, rhythm consistent
- Cover text legible as a thumbnail; on-image text under 60 characters per slide
- Caption under 1000 characters with the concrete lived details and keywords
- No external links, no absolute-claim 违禁词 (slides included)
- Ends with one takeaway plus one interaction hook
