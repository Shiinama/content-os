# Jike (即刻)

Default output language: Chinese.

## Audience & mindset
A small, dense community of builders, product people, and internet-native professionals. People post as themselves, in real time, about what they are actually doing and thinking. Authenticity and specificity are the currency; polish is suspicious.

## Format & length
Short post, ideally 100 to 500 characters. Longer only when the content genuinely earns it. Casual screenshots and photos raise engagement. Posts go into 圈子 (topic circles); picking the right circle is part of the post.

## Visuals
- The deliberate exception: generated polish is off-brand here — polish is suspicious. Default is no generated images; a real screenshot or photo beats anything produced.
- Generate only when the user explicitly asks, and keep it casual, never cover-grade.

## Structure norms
- Lead with the observation or feeling, not background. Jike reads like thinking out loud, mid-process.
- One thought per post. If there are three thoughts, that is three posts.
- Work-in-progress beats retrospective: "我刚发现" outperforms "上个月我总结了".

## Tone & information density
First person, conversational, unguarded. It is fine to be uncertain, to ask, to complain with humor. Medium-low density: leave room for comments to complete the thought. Emojis natural, not performative.

## Hooks & titles
No titles. The first sentence is a hook only in the sense that it is genuinely interesting: a fresh observation, a small win with numbers, a contrarian feeling about a common tool. Anything that smells like content marketing dies instantly here.

## CTA norms
None, or a genuine question. The community rewards posts that invite conversation, not ones that ask for it.

## Taboos & failure modes
- Cross-posted marketing copy from other platforms is spotted immediately and ignored.
- Long essays underperform; link out or split instead.
- Engagement-bait questions ("你们觉得呢？" appended to an ad) read as fake.
- Posting outside the right 圈子 wastes the post.

## Pre-publish checklist
- Under 500 characters, one thought
- Reads like something said to peers mid-work, not published content
- A concrete detail or number that makes it real
- Suggested 圈子 named for the user
- No marketing smell
